# Cloud Kitchen P&L Intelligence Dashboard

Business Intelligence & Financial Analytics System for Cloud Kitchens

---

## Overview

The Cloud Kitchen P&L Intelligence Dashboard is a business analytics solution designed to analyze the financial and operational performance of cloud kitchens across multiple stores, cities, and operational zones.

This project transforms raw kitchen-level P&L data into actionable business insights through interactive dashboards, KPI tracking, variance intelligence, and profitability analytics.

The solution helps stakeholders:

- Monitor operational efficiency
- Track EBITDA and contribution margins
- Detect inventory and operational leakages
- Analyze city-wise and store-wise performance
- Identify profitable and loss-making kitchens
- Make data-driven strategic decisions

---

## Business Problem

Cloud kitchen businesses operate with:

- Thin operational margins
- High inventory sensitivity
- Dynamic demand fluctuations
- Operational inefficiencies
- Wastage and leakage risks

Traditional reporting systems often fail to provide centralized visibility into profitability and operational performance.

This project addresses that challenge by building a centralized analytics platform capable of monitoring financial health and operational efficiency across all kitchen locations.

## Understanding Key Business Metrics

### Net Revenue

Net Revenue represents the total sales generated by a kitchen.

Example:

```text
Revenue = Total value of all food orders
```

It helps measure:

- Business scale
- Customer demand
- Growth performance

---

### Gross Margin (GM)

Gross Margin shows how much money remains after direct operational costs such as:

- Ingredients
- Packaging
- Raw materials

Formula:

```text
Gross Margin = Revenue - Direct Costs
```

Higher gross margin indicates better operational efficiency.

---

### Gross Margin %

Gross Margin % measures contribution efficiency relative to revenue.

Formula:

```text
GM% = Gross Margin / Revenue × 100
```

This helps compare kitchens fairly regardless of their size.

---

### EBITDA

EBITDA represents the actual operational profitability of the kitchen.

It accounts for expenses like:

- Salaries
- Rent
- Utilities
- Operations
- Staff expenses

Formula (simplified):

```text
EBITDA = Revenue - Operational Expenses
```

Positive EBITDA means the kitchen is profitable.

Negative EBITDA means the kitchen is operating at a loss.

---

### EBITDA %

EBITDA % measures how efficiently revenue converts into profit.

Formula:

```text
EBITDA% = EBITDA / Revenue × 100
```

Higher EBITDA% indicates stronger operational efficiency.

---

### Variance

Variance represents the mismatch between expected and actual operational values.

It may indicate:

- Inventory leakage
- Food wastage
- Procurement inefficiencies
- Forecasting issues
- Operational inefficiency

Example:

```text
Expected Cost = ₹5,00,000
Actual Cost = ₹5,30,000

Variance = ₹30,000
```

Higher variance is generally undesirable.

---

### Variance %

Variance % measures operational mismatch relative to revenue.

Formula:

```text
Variance% = Variance / Revenue × 100
```

Lower variance percentages indicate healthier operations.
